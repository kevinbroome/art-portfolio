About

The work

Figurative, distorted, emotional, and slightly feral. The nice polite gallery wall got dragged through a dream and came back different.

I make modern neo-expressionist digital work focused on fractured bodies, uneasy colour, and the strange theatre of being a person. The images sit somewhere between confession, mask, and omen.
